Gordian Labs was formed back in late 2006 as a boutique software development and consultancy firm. It was co-founded by five close friends and fellow techies who loved working together and building cool stuff, mostly in fintech: <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/rjsjr/"  target="_new">Robert Seymour</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/andycarra/" target="_new">Andy Carra</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/john-wolthuis-2493a61/" target="_new">John Wolthuis</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/hrishi-dixit/" target="_new">Hrishi Dixit  (me)</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/billmerrill/" target="_new">Bill Merrill</a>. 

We did several projects for financial and other institutions like Thomson Reuters, Bear Stearns, NYU, BountyJobs, MySQL, and others. Those paid the bills. What we particularly enjoyed was 0-to-1 bootstraps of tech startups, which we lovingly called "tech incubations" or "bench equity" gigs (these were often paid purely in equity). These didn't always pay the bills, but they were a blast to work on. Occasionally, some worked out well, like <a href="https://learnvest.com" target="_new">LearnVest</a>, or <a href="https://twilio.com" target="_new">Twilio</a>! In fact, one of the Gordian founding partners went on to become a <a href="https://www.crunchbase.com/person/john-wolthuis" target="_new"> Twilio co-founder</a>.

The old Gordian Labs ceased active operations in 2010 in the wake of the Great Recession, as its principals moved on to co-found or take leadership roles in various startups like LearnVest, <a href="https://sofi.com" target="_new">SoFi</a>, Sindeo, and <a href="https://www.yieldstreet.com" target="_new">Yieldstreet</a>. 

The new Gordian Labs was resurrected as a single-member (me) advisory and seed investing firm in late 2024, 18 years after its original founding. 

And that is the brief history of Gordian Labs.
